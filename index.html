Welcome to Chap's OnFood

Skip the line. Enjoy the food.

Chap's OnFood is the official online pre-order platform for the LCU Cafeteria. Instead of waiting in a long line during busy hours, you can now browse the menu, place your order online, and simply show up at your chosen pickup time. Your meal will be ready and waiting for you.

This service is available exclusively for LCU students. All you need is your student ID and a couple of minutes to place your order.

How It Works

Follow these three simple steps to pre-order your meal:

  1. Browse the Menu – Head over to the Menu page to see today's available dishes, descriptions, and prices.
  2. Place Your Order – Go to the Order page, fill in your student information, select your items, and choose a pickup time.
  3. Pick Up & Enjoy – Show up at the cafeteria at your chosen time. No waiting in line — your tray is already prepared!

Why Use Chap's OnFood?

Here are a few reasons why students love pre-ordering their meals:

  • Save Time – Pre-ordering takes less than 3 minutes and saves you 15–20 minutes of waiting.
  • Easy to Use – No app download needed. Works in any browser on any device.
  • Plan Your Day – Choose your pickup time so your meal fits around your class schedule.
  • Always Fresh – Your food is prepared right before your pickup time, not hours in advance.

Quick Info

Feature Details
Who can order? LCU students only (valid student ID required)
Order deadline At least 30 minutes before your pickup time
Pickup location LCU Cafeteria front counter
