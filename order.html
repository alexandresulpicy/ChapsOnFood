Before You Order

Please follow these instructions to ensure a smooth pickup experience:

  1. Browse the Menu page and note the items you want.
  2. Fill in your student information accurately in the form below.
  3. Select your meal items and specify the quantity for each.
  4. Choose your pickup time — at least 30 minutes from now.
  5. Click Submit Order and wait for confirmation at the counter.

Need help? Contact us at chapsonline@lcu.edu.
